# AI Governance & Risk

This directory contains governance, risk-management, and compliance exercises related to Agentic AI systems.

The purpose is to explore how technical AI security controls connect to organizational risk and governance requirements.

Topics may include:

* AI system inventories
* AI risk assessments
* AI risk registers
* Human oversight
* AI incident response
* AI acceptable-use policies
* Third-party AI risk
* Model and agent documentation
* Control design
* Evidence management
* AI lifecycle governance
* Regulatory considerations

## Frameworks

Research and projects may reference frameworks and standards such as:

* NIST AI Risk Management Framework
* NIST Cybersecurity Framework
* ISO/IEC 42001
* MITRE ATLAS
* OWASP guidance for AI and LLM systems
* Relevant AI regulatory requirements

Framework mappings created in this repository are educational exercises unless otherwise stated.

## Goal

The goal is to understand not only whether an Agentic AI system works, but whether it can be:

**explained, controlled, monitored, audited, risk-assessed, and governed responsibly.**
